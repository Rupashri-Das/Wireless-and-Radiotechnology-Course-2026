# Short Answers (Lecture-2)

## Q1
The time domain shows how a signal changes with time. It helps us observe the waveform and amplitude variations. The frequency domain shows which frequencies exist in the signal and their strengths. It is obtained using Fourier Transform or FFT. In RF systems, the frequency domain is useful to analyze signal components and bandwidth.

## Q2
Filtering helps RF receivers separate the desired signal from other unwanted signals or noise. In communication systems many signals exist at different frequencies. Filters allow only specific frequencies to pass and block others. This improves signal quality and reduces interference. Therefore filtering is an important step in RF receivers.

## Q3
Modulation moves a low frequency signal to a higher frequency so it can be transmitted through antennas efficiently. It allows multiple signals to share the same communication channel using different frequencies. Modulation also improves transmission distance. In RF systems it converts baseband signals to passband signals.

## Q4
The easiest filter to design was the low-pass filter for extracting the 500 Hz signal. This is because we only needed to set a cutoff frequency slightly above 500 Hz. The filter automatically removes higher frequencies like 600, 700, and 800 Hz. Therefore it is simple and effective for isolating the lowest frequency component.

## Q5
After modulation, new frequency components appear at the sum and difference of the carrier and message frequencies. In this assignment the message frequency was 100 Hz and the carrier was 2000 Hz. Therefore the new components appear around 1900 Hz and 2100 Hz. These are called sidebands in amplitude modulation.
